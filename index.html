Features

Everything a tooth fairy fan needs

Made for families, with a gentle, kid-friendly design and a secret parent zone.

📸

Capture the Moment

Snap a photo of the tooth on the pillow and a photo of your room. The fairy finds the spot and flies in.

🧚

Magical Fairy Visits

A fully animated Tooth Fairy flutters into your own room, takes the tooth, and leaves her magic behind.

💰

Fairy Bank & Rewards

Parents set the reward for each tooth. Kids watch their savings grow and work toward a goal.

📜

Keepsake Certificates

Print or save a beautiful certificate with the tooth, the date, and the age. A memory to treasure.

🎮

Play With Your Fairy

Tap anywhere and your fairy friend and her pet fly right to you. Customize her look, wings, dress, and skin tone.

🦷

Learn & Play

Friendly dental facts, daily care tips, and fun activities that make healthy habits stick.

🔒

Parent Zone

A PIN-protected area for reward amounts, visit times, and notifications. Settings hidden from kids.

🎨

Your Colors

Four color themes: Midnight, Ocean, Forest, and Sunset. Pick the one that feels like you.

Version 2.0

What's New in Version 2.0

A brand-new fairy, a brand-new look, and keepsakes to treasure.

✨

Meet Your New Tooth Fairy!

She now has a full body, a real face, a sparkling gown, and fluttering butterfly wings. Watch her fly into your room, collect your tooth, and leave your reward.

📜

Tooth Certificates

Make a fancy keepsake certificate for every lost tooth. Choose which tooth it was, set the date and age, and add the tooth photo. Print it or save it as a PDF.

🎨

Four Color Themes

Choose Midnight, Ocean, Forest, or Sunset. Your colors show up across the whole app.

🌙

A Magical New Dark Look

The whole app has moved from a bright white background to a rich midnight design that's easier on the eyes at bedtime.

🧚

Customize Your Fairy's Skin Tone

Pick a skin tone that looks like you, and your fairy appears that way throughout the app.

🦷

Easier to Read, Smoother to Use

The Daily Dental Care tips are now clear and readable. Home and Capture Your Room no longer bounce around, and the fairy's wings stay attached to her body.

🔒

Parent Zone Updates

It's easier to read, with a Support & Help Center link and a quick way to rate the app.

Thank you for sharing the magic with your family! 💜
