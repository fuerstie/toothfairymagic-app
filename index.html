Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Your privacy, and the privacy of your children, is of the utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy details how the Tooth Fairy Magic application handles information.

1. Information Collection and Use

Tooth Fairy Magic is built with maximum data privacy in mind. The app does not collect, harvest, or transmit any personal information to external servers. All data entered into the app—including profile customizaton, tooth milestone tracking, and PIN codes—is stored strictly locally on your physical device.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Because our application is designed for families and children, we strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect, request, or store any personal information from children under the age of 13. No data leaves the device, ensuring a completely safe offline environment.

3. Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with third-party analytics platforms, tracking software, or advertising networks. Your family’s usage remains private to you.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all app data resides safely on your device's local storage, deleting the Tooth Fairy Magic app from your device will instantly and permanently erase all data associated with it.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this privacy policy, please contact us at our dedicated support address.
