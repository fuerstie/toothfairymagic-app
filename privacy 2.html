Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your privacy, and the privacy of your children, is the most important thing to us. This policy explains how the Tooth Fairy Magic app handles information. The short version: the app does not collect or send your information anywhere. It all stays on your device.

1. Information collection and use

Tooth Fairy Magic does not collect, harvest, sell, or transmit personal information to us or to any external server. Information you enter or create in the app stays on your device. This includes:

  • Your child's name, age, and fairy customization (skin tone, wing and dress colors, pet).
  • Tooth records (the date and which tooth), reward amounts, and Fairy Bank savings and goals.
  • Your Parent PIN and parent settings, such as visit times and notification options.
  • Your app color theme choice.

2. Camera and photos

The app asks for camera access so you can take a photo of a lost tooth and a photo of your room. These photos are stored on your device inside the app. They are used to show the fairy visit in your room and to put the tooth photo on a keepsake certificate. They are never uploaded or shared by the app.

3. Notifications

If a parent turns on Fairy Visit Notifications, the app schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. These notifications are created and delivered on your device. No notification service of ours is involved, and nothing is sent to a server.

4. Certificates, printing, and sharing

When you create a tooth certificate, the app generates a PDF on your device. If you choose to print it, save it, or share it (for example by email or AirDrop), that is your choice and it goes where you send it. The app doesn't send certificates anywhere on its own.

5. Children's privacy (COPPA)

The app is made for families and children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13, or from anyone. Because no data leaves the device, there is nothing for us to collect, store, or share. Settings that are meant for adults are behind a Parent PIN.

6. Third-party services, ads, and tracking

The app has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Apple may collect information about App Store downloads and ratings under Apple's own privacy policy. We do not control that and don't receive personal information from it.

7. Data retention and deletion

All data lives in the app on your device. Deleting Tooth Fairy Magic from your device removes all of its data, including photos, tooth records, savings, and settings. Parents can also edit or change information inside the app at any time.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.
