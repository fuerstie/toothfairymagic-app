Frequently asked questions

How does a fairy visit work?

Take a photo of the tooth, and a photo of your room with the pillow outline lined up on your pillow. Then open Watch Fairy Visit and tap Play. By default parents can have the visit wait until morning, so the fairy only visits while kids sleep.

How do I change how much the fairy leaves?

Open My Fairy Profile, tap the lock, and enter the Parent PIN. In Parent Settings choose Fairy Reward and edit the amount per tooth.

Can the fairy wait until morning?

Yes. In Parent Settings, turn on Lock visit until morning and pick the time the visit becomes available. You can also turn on a Fairy Visit Notification with your own message and time.

How do I make a tooth certificate?

Open Watch Fairy Visit and tap the ribbon button next to a tooth. Choose which tooth it was, set the date and age, then tap Print Certificate, or Save or Share as PDF.

How do I change the app colors?

Open My Fairy Profile and scroll to App Colors. Pick Midnight, Ocean, Forest, or Sunset. The change shows up across the whole app.

How do I change my fairy's look and skin tone?

On My Fairy Profile tap Customize Fairy Appearance. You can pick skin tone, wing color, dress color, and a fairy pet, with a live preview.

Where is my family's information stored?

Only on your device. The app doesn't send photos, names, or settings to any server. See the Privacy Policy for details.

I forgot my Parent PIN or something isn't working.

Email kory.fuerstenberg@hotmail.com and we'll help you out. Please mention your device and iOS version.
